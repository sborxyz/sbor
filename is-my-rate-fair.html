How to compare fairly

  1. Same stablecoin. USDC, USDT and other dollar stablecoins are different loans with different rates. Compare with the rate for yours.
  2. Fixed or variable. SBOR's daily rate is variable. For a fixed rate, or a loan that runs for months, compare with the 30, 90 or 180 day average, and expect some premium for fixing.
  3. Bitcoin only. SBOR counts markets where plain bitcoin is the only collateral. A loan against a pool of assets is priced differently.
  4. All-in cost. Add fees to the rate. The loan-to-value ratio, the liquidation terms and who holds your bitcoin matter as much as the rate.

Questions

What is SBOR?
The Standard Bitcoin Offered Rate: the daily reference rate for borrowing dollars against bitcoin. It reads every eligible lending market straight from its contract, publishes one dated rate with every market behind it, and posts it on-chain on five chains. The way SOFR works for dollars backed by Treasuries.
Can I trust the number?
Every fixing is a public commit, published fixings are never rewritten, and every correction is logged in public. The method is versioned and self-assessed against the IOSCO Principles for Financial Benchmarks. SBOR has no token and never trades on its own rate.
Does SBOR lend, or recommend lenders?
No. SBOR publishes a statistic. It holds no money, routes no loans and recommends no one.
