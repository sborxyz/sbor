SBOR on Stacks

Every morning, after the fixing, SBOR posts its benchmark lending rates to a contract on Stacks mainnet, so any Stacks contract, application or agent can read them on-chain. The table below is read live from Stacks by your browser, not from SBOR's servers. The same rates are also posted on Arc, Base, Hyperliquid and Solana.

Rates are effective annual rates, posted in basis points. SBOR-USD, SBOR-BTC and SBOR-STX are SBOR's indices for lending on Stacks; the bitcoin-collateral USDC rate is a reference covering Morpho markets on Base, Ethereum and Arc. Sizes are total supplied, in US dollars. An index not published on a given day keeps its last date on-chain.
