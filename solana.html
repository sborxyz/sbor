SBOR on Solana

Every morning, after the fixing, SBOR posts its benchmark lending rates on Solana mainnet, as a signed record from SBOR's publisher, so any Solana application, vault or agent can read them from the chain. The table below is read live from Solana by your browser, not from SBOR's servers. The same rates are also posted on Stacks, Arc and Base.

Rates are effective annual rates, posted in basis points. SBOR-USD, SBOR-BTC and SBOR-STX are SBOR's indices for lending on Stacks; the bitcoin-collateral USDC rate is a reference covering Morpho markets on Base, Ethereum and Arc. Sizes are total supplied, in US dollars. An index not published on a given day keeps its last date on-chain.

Publisher on Solana mainnet:
Each fixing is a memo beginning {"sbor":1, with each rate as [borrowBps, supplyBps, depthUsd]. To read it: the publisher's recent transactions, newest first, through any Solana RPC. Source: scripts/solana-publish.mjs · Methodology: sbor.xyz
