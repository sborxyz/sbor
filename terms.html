What SBOR is

SBOR is a free benchmark of lending rates for Bitcoin DeFi, read from public lending contracts and published once a day, with reference rates beside it. No account, key or payment is needed.

Market data, not advice

SBOR publishes market data. It is not financial, investment, legal or tax advice, and it does not recommend any transaction. A comparison against the benchmark, including the default suggestion to stop and ask a human when a borrowing rate is more than 50 basis points above it, is information to help you decide. The decision stays with you.
